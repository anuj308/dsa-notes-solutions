Unit 1
Ethics
🔹 Definition & Importance

Ethics refers to moral principles governing behavior, while integrity means adherence to honesty and consistency. In business, ethics builds trust and long-term success.

🔹 Creating an Ethical Work Environment
✅ Knowledgeable resource
Employees must have a trusted person to report unethical practices.
✅ Clear policies & training
Written standards + regular ethics training.
✅ Manager commitment
Leaders model ethical behavior and enforce accountability.
✅ Reporting channels
Hotlines, open-door policies without fear of retaliation.
🔹 Ethical Decision-Making Process
  1. Develop a problem statement – define who is affected, impact, frequency.
  2. Identify alternatives – brainstorm with stakeholders without early criticism.
  3. Evaluate & choose – weigh consequences, ethical principles.
  4. Implement decision – efficient, timely, communicate “why”.
  5. Evaluate results – check success criteria & unintended outcomes.
🔹 IT Ethics & Professional Behavior

Common ethical issues for IT users: privacy concerns, cybersecurity risks, misuse of IT resources, surveillance & monitoring. The Business Software Alliance (BSA) fights software piracy with over 100 lawyers and investigators.

📌 Supporting ethical practices of IT users:
Education & awareness, clear policies, access control, ethical use of AI, collaboration.
Unit 2
Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)
📌 Types of Intellectual Property
TypeProtection scope
PatentExclusive rights for inventions (20 years term, territorial). Novelty required.
CopyrightOriginal literary, artistic works (novels, music, films, website content).
TrademarkSigns, logos, brand names distinguishing goods/services. ® for registered marks.
Trade SecretConfidential business info with commercial value (secrecy + reasonable protection).
Geographical Indication (GI)Identifies product origin (e.g., Darjeeling tea).
DesignVisual appearance (shape, color, pattern).
🏷️ Special Trademarks
  • Certification marks – UL, Energy Star, Organic certification.
  • Shape marks – 3D product configuration.
  • Pattern marks – Gucci GG, Louis Vuitton monogram.
  • Sound marks – distinctive audio identifiers.
  • Service marks (SM) – identify services (FedEx, Netflix).
  • Collective marks – owned by an organization for members.
📄 Patent Process & Filing

Who can file? True & first inventor, assignee, legal representative. Types of applications: ordinary, convention, PCT national phase, divisional, patent of addition.

Provisional specification → early priority (12 months to file complete). Complete specification must contain title, field, background, objects, summary, drawings, claims.

⚙️ Stages from filing to grant: filing → publication (18 months) → request for examination (48 months from filing) → examiner report (1-3 months) → grant or opposition. Renewal fees required yearly.

Loss of rights: improper use, genericide, non-use, failure to police, expiry. Litigation vs licensing: litigation stops competitor, licensing earns royalties.

Unit 3
Government Funding & Startup Schemes
🇮🇳 Bank Loans & Financial Support
  • MUDRA (PMMY) – up to ₹10 lakh, no collateral. Shishu (₹50k), Kishor (₹5L), Tarun (₹10L).
  • Stand-Up India – ₹10 lakh to ₹1 crore, for SC/ST/women entrepreneurs (51% ownership).
  • CGTMSE – collateral-free loans up to ₹2 crore for MSMEs.
  • SIDBI Startup Loan – ₹10 lakh – ₹10 crore, innovation focus.
  • Bank-specific – SBI Startup Loan, HDFC Business Growth, ICICI, Axis.
🚀 Startup India – Benefits (DPIIT recognised)
Tax Holiday
100% tax exemption for 3 consecutive years (Section 80-IAC).
Patent & IPR
80% rebate on patent fees, 50% on trademark, fast-track examination.
Self-Certification
Compliance for 9 labour & environmental laws.
Fund of Funds
₹10,000 crore via SIDBI for VC investment.

Eligibility: less than 10 years old, turnover ≤ ₹100 crore, innovation-driven, not formed by splitting existing business.

🌱 NASSCOM 10,000 Startups Initiative

Launched 2013, focus on AI, FinTech, HealthTech, SaaS. Over 7,500 startups incubated, $4B+ funding raised. Success examples: Freshworks, Unacademy, Razorpay.

📦 Export Promotion: STP & SEZ
  • STP (Software Technology Parks) – 10-year tax holiday, 100% FDI, duty-free imports, ~45% of IT exports.
  • SEZ (Special Economic Zones) – 15-year corporate tax holiday, 270+ operational SEZs, ₹7.5 lakh crore exports (2022-23).
Unit 4
Startup in IT – Business Plan
📋 Key Components of an IT Business Plan
  • Executive Summary – startup overview, industry niche, business model (SaaS, consulting, custom solutions).
  • General Company Description – name, location, legal structure (LLC/ Pvt Ltd), founders.
  • Products & Services – custom software, AI automation, cloud, cybersecurity.
  • Marketing Plan – digital strategy, target market analysis, buyer personas.
  • Operational Plan – workflows, agile methodologies, resource allocation, risk management.
  • Management & Organization – founding team, hierarchy, decision authority.
  • Personal Financial Statement – net worth of owners, assets outside business.
  • Startup Expenses & Capitalization – itemised costs, total investment needed.
  • Financial Plan – 12-month P&L, cash flow projection, break-even analysis.
  • Appendices – brochures, leases, letters of support, market studies.
✍️ Refining the Plan for Different Audiences
For Bankers
Loan amount, collateral, repayment terms.
For Investors
ROI, funds needed in 2-5 years, scaling.
Manufacturing
Production levels, inventory, pricing.
Service businesses
Quality control, subcontracting, pricing flexibility.
📈 Startup Stories: Flipkart (started with books, introduced COD, acquired Myntra) and MakeMyTrip (Deep Kalra, transformed online travel booking). Inspiration for IT entrepreneurs.

NASSCOM – National Association of Software & Service Companies; represents India’s IT-BPM industry, policy making, skill development, global collaborations.
STPI (Software Technology Parks of India) – supports software exports, provides incubation, tax benefits, infrastructure, key role in India’s IT growth.

🌍 Latest Global IT Projects & Trends
  • Aadhaar (India’s digital ID), Google AI-driven search, AWS cloud, Tesla self-driving, Paytm fintech, OpenAI ChatGPT.
  • Emerging tech: AI/ML growth, 5G, blockchain, quantum computing (Amazon Ocelot chip).

Tech industry challenges: women hold ~28% of tech jobs, underrepresentation of Black/Hispanic professionals. Efforts toward inclusive hiring, but leadership gaps persist.

Unit 6
Legal, Ethical & Professional Issues in Information Security
⚖️ Organizational Liability & Legal Counsel

Organizations can be held liable for data breaches, regulatory non-compliance (GDPR, HIPAA, PCI DSS), cybersecurity negligence, IP violations, insider threats. Legal counsel ensures compliance, data protection policies, incident defense, and risk mitigation.

📌 Real cases: Facebook-Cambridge Analytica, Equifax breach (unpatched Apache Struts), Uber cover-up, Yahoo breach.
📜 Policy vs Law
  • Policy – internal guidelines, enforced by management, flexible (e.g., AUP, password policy, BYOD).
  • Law – government-enforced rules, legal penalties (e.g., IT Act, GDPR, CFAA).
🇮🇳 Cyber Law in India

IT Act, 2000 – primary cyber law covering hacking, identity theft, phishing, data protection. Penalties up to 10 years imprisonment. IPC sections: 419/420 (online fraud), 500 (cyber defamation), 354D (cyberstalking). Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 – regulates data privacy, strict penalties for breaches. National Cyber Security Policy, 2013 – protects critical sectors.

📈 Cybercrime on the Rise

Expected global damage $10.5 trillion annually by 2025. Attack every 39 seconds. Over 50,000 cases registered in India (2023). Reasons: digital dependence, lack of awareness, advanced hacking, financial motivation.

🛡️ Cybersecurity Tools & Best Practices
Network tools
Wireshark, Nmap, Snort
Penetration testing
Metasploit, Burp Suite, Kali Linux
EDR & SIEM
CrowdStrike, Splunk, IBM QRadar
Forensics

Best practices: MFA, regular patching, employee awareness, strong passwords, backup, zero-trust principles, incident response plan. Case studies: Colonial Pipeline ransomware, phishing wire fraud ($100M loss), Equifax breach – emphasize timely updates.
