📝 Unit 1: Ethics

Quick Notes

Definition of Ethics

  • Ethics = moral principles that govern a person's behavior or conduct
  • Derived from Greek word "ethos" meaning character
  • Helps distinguish between right and wrong

Importance of Integrity

  • Integrity = consistency of actions, values, methods & principles
  • Builds trust and credibility
  • Essential for long-term professional success

Ethics in Business World

  • Corporate ethics = applying ethical principles in business context
  • Covers fair treatment, transparency, accountability
  • Improves corporate image and stakeholder trust

Improving Corporate Ethics

  • Establish clear code of conduct
  • Provide ethics training programs
  • Lead by example from top management
  • Create reporting mechanisms for unethical behavior

Creating an Ethical Work Environment

  • Employees need a knowledgeable resource to discuss ethical concerns
  • Manager's checklist: set clear expectations, model ethical behavior, reward integrity
  • Open-door policy for ethical discussions

Ethical Decision Making

  • Develop a Problem Statement: What is wrong? Who is affected? How serious?
  • Identify Alternatives: Brainstorm multiple solutions
  • Evaluate & Choose: Assess consequences, pick best option
  • Implement: Efficient, effective, timely execution
  • Evaluate Results: Success criteria met? Unintended consequences?

Ethics in Information Technology

  • Privacy concerns, cybersecurity risks, misuse of IT resources
  • Copyright violation (music, films, software)
  • Spam emails, hacking, plagiarism, tracking
  • Ethical use of AI and automation

Ethical Behavior of IT Professional

  • Profession: Requires specialized knowledge & intensive academic preparation
  • Professional Employee: Exercises discretion & judgment
  • Key traits: specialized knowledge, ethical behavior, accountability, commitment to continuous learning

Common Ethical Issues for IT Users

  • Privacy concerns, Cybersecurity risks, Misuse of IT resources, Surveillance and monitoring

Business Software Alliance (BSA)

  • Trade group representing world's largest software & hardware manufacturers
  • Mission: stop unauthorized copying of software
  • Over 100 lawyers & investigators prosecute piracy cases

Supporting Ethical Practices of IT Users

  • Education & awareness programs
  • Clear policies & standards, Access control & security
  • Ethical use of AI & automation, Women empowerment in tech

📝 Unit 2: Intellectual Properties

Quick Notes

Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)

  • Legal rights that protect creations of the mind
  • Encourage development & distribution of new products/services
  • Granted over inventions, designs, trademarks, creative content

Types of IP

TypeProtects
PatentInventions — exclusive rights for 20 years
CopyrightOriginal creative works (novels, art, music, films, code)
TrademarkSigns distinguishing goods/services (logos, names)
Trade SecretConfidential business information
Geographical Indication (GI)Products from a specific place using traditional methods
DesignVisual appearance (shape, color, pattern, texture)
Plant Variety (PVR)New plant varieties from unauthorized reproduction

Patents — Detailed

  • Statutory grant by government for new inventions
  • Monopoly Rights: Exclusive rights for 20 years from filing date
  • Novelty: Subject matter must NOT be published anywhere in the world prior to filing
  • Territorial: Patent rights are country-specific
  • Patentable: Process/method, machine/apparatus, article of manufacture, composition of matter, improvements
  • Types of applications: Ordinary, Convention, Patent of Addition, Divisional, PCT International, PCT National

Provisional vs Complete Specification

  • Provisional: Early priority, less expensive, no claims, no abstract
  • Complete: Must be filed within 12 months of provisional filing
  • Contents: Title, Field, Background (Prior Art), Object, Summary, Drawings, Detailed Description, Examples, Claims, Abstract

Who Can File a Patent? (Section 6)

  • True and first inventor
  • Assignee of true and first inventor
  • Legal representative of deceased person entitled to apply

Patent Grant Stages

  • Publication and Examination
  • Examination request: 48 months from filing/priority date
  • Controller refers to Examiner within 1 month
  • Examiner prepares report in 1-3 months
  • Renewal fees paid every year till end of term

Types of Trademarks

  • Product Mark: Identifies goods/products (e.g., Pepsi, Nike Swoosh)
  • Service Mark: Identifies services (SM symbol, e.g., FedEx)
  • Certification Mark: Defines standard (UL, Fair Trade, Energy Star)
  • Collective Mark: Owned by a collective/group
  • Shape Mark: Protects 3D shape of product or packaging
  • Pattern Mark: Specific pattern/design (Gucci GG, Louis Vuitton)
  • Sound Mark: Protects specific sound (e.g., Netflix intro)

Trademark — Loss of Rights & Benefits

  • Loss: Improper use, genericide, non-use, improper assignment, failure to police/renew
  • Benefits: Prima facie evidence, exclusive right, assignable, perpetual goodwill with renewal

Trade Secret Requirements

  • Secrecy, Commercial Value, Reasonable Efforts to keep secret

Litigation vs Licensing

  • Litigation: Stop competitor from infringing — costly, timely, disruptive
  • Licensing: Obtain royalty for use of invention
  • Alternatives: Mediation & Arbitration (ADR)

📝 Unit 3: Government Funding & Startup Schemes

Quick Notes

What is a Startup? (DPIIT Definition)

  • Company in existence for less than 10 years
  • Annual turnover ≤ ₹100 crore
  • Working on innovation, development, or improvement of products/processes/services
  • Scalable business model with high employment & wealth creation potential
  • Should NOT be formed by splitting/reconstructing existing business

Startup India Initiative (Launched Jan 16, 2016)

  • Aims: Promote entrepreneurship, innovation, job creation
  • Over 100,000 startups recognized by DPIIT
  • India = 3rd largest startup ecosystem globally

Startup India — Benefits

  • Tax Holiday: 100% tax exemption for 3 consecutive years (Section 80-IAC)
  • Capital Gains Tax Exemption: Reinvestment in approved funds
  • Self-Certification: For 9 labor & environmental laws
  • Fast-Track Patent: 80% reduction in patent filing fees
  • Fund of Funds (FFS): ₹10,000 crore managed by SIDBI

MUDRA Loan (PMMY)

CategoryAmountPurpose
ShishuUp to ₹50,000New businesses
Kishor₹50,000 - ₹5 lakhGrowing businesses
Tarun₹5 lakh - ₹10 lakhEstablished businesses
No collateral required | Interest: 8%-12%

Other Loan Schemes

  • Stand-Up India: ₹10 lakh - ₹1 crore (Women/SC/ST, 51% ownership)
  • CGTMSE: Up to ₹2 crore (collateral-free, MSMEs)
  • SIDBI: ₹10 lakh - ₹10 crore (innovation, technology)

NASSCOM — 10,000 Startups Initiative (2013)

  • 7,500+ startups incubated, 300+ scaled globally, $4B+ funding raised
  • Success: Razorpay, Unacademy, Freshworks (1st SaaS unicorn), Digit Insurance
  • Future: Tier-2/3 cities, AI/DeepTech/Sustainability

STP Scheme (Software Technology Parks) — 1991

  • 10-year income tax exemption, duty-free imports, 100% FDI
  • ~4.5 million jobs, ~45% of IT exports

SEZ Scheme (Special Economic Zones) — 2005

  • 15-year corporate tax holiday, duty-free import/export
  • 270+ SEZs, ₹7.5 lakh crore exports, 2+ million jobs
  • 20% of India's total exports

📝 Unit 4: Startup in IT

Quick Notes

Executive Summary

  • Overview of the startup & its industry
  • Business model: Subscription, SaaS, Consulting, Custom solutions
  • Target market: Small businesses, enterprises, healthcare, fintech
  • Key offerings: Custom software, AI automation, cloud, cybersecurity, IT consulting
  • Goals: Launch MVP, acquire clients, expand team, achieve revenue growth
  • Financial overview: Initial investment, revenue streams, projected ROI

General Company Description

  • Company name, founded date, location, business structure
  • Mission, Vision, Core values (Innovation, Customer-Centric, Integrity, Scalability)

Marketing Plan

  • Target market analysis, Digital strategy (paid ads, email, social media)
  • Customer engagement (AI chatbots, feedback), Performance measurement (KPIs, ROI)

Operational Plan

  • Business process management, Resource allocation, Technology infrastructure
  • Risk management & compliance, Performance monitoring

Product-Based vs Service-Based IT Companies

Product-BasedService-Based
Develop & sell software productsOffer IT services & consulting
Revenue: licensing & subscriptionsRevenue: contracts & projects
Examples: Microsoft, Google, AdobeExamples: TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture

Financial Plan

  • Opening Day Balance Sheet, Break-Even Analysis, Cash Flow
  • Startup Expenses & Capitalization

Appendices & Refining the Plan

  • Brochures, studies, maps, equipment lists, leases, contracts
  • For bankers: Loan amount, repayment terms, collateral
  • For investors: Short-term & long-term needs, ROI projections

Case Study — Flipkart

  • Founded 2007 by Sachin Bansal & Binny Bansal (IIT Delhi, ex-Amazon)
  • Started with books, introduced COD in India
  • Acquired Myntra, LetsBuy. Valued at ~$15 billion

Case Study — MakeMyTrip

  • Founded by Deep Kalra (IIM-A), based in Gurgaon
  • Pioneered online travel booking in India
